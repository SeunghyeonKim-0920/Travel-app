## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Three isolated travel design concepts are available
The site SHALL provide three visually distinct, travel-focused design previews without replacing the current production theme.

#### Scenario: User compares concepts
- **WHEN** the user opens the design preview gallery
- **THEN** three named concepts can be viewed with representative navigation, itinerary controls, destination imagery, and travel content

### Requirement: Design previews adapt to common viewports
Each preview SHALL fit desktop, portrait-phone, and landscape-phone viewports without horizontal clipping or overlapping controls.

#### Scenario: Mobile comparison
- **WHEN** a concept is viewed at 390x844 or 844x390
- **THEN** navigation, primary content, and controls remain visible and usable within the viewport
