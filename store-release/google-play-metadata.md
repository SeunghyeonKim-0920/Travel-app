## Full description

Plan an independent trip with practical day-by-day schedules. TripTogether creates itineraries for supported cities, shows places on a map, helps compare routes across several cities, and lets you save or share the result.

Use companion matching when you want company for a specific part of the trip. Browse room details before joining, review public profile preferences, and chat inside the app.

Features include:

- Itinerary creation with selectable travel pace
- Multi-city route planning
- Shareable trip and route links
- Companion rooms and chat
- Six interface languages
- PDF and offline-friendly output

Transport, opening-hour, fare, and availability details can change. Check official providers before booking or travel.
