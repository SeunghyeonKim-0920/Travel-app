## Description

TripTogether helps independent travelers organize a trip in one place. Create a day-by-day itinerary for supported destinations, adjust the pace, save and share plans, and compare routes between multiple cities. When you want company for part of the trip, browse or create companion rooms and chat before meeting.

Key features:

- Day-by-day itinerary creation with maps and travel-time guidance
- Multi-city route planning and shareable plans
- Companion rooms with profile preferences and in-room chat
- Korean, English, French, Chinese, Japanese, and Spanish interfaces
- PDF and offline-friendly trip information

Route, opening-hour, fare, and availability information can change. Confirm time-sensitive details with the official provider before travel.

## Review notes

Test the itinerary planner without an account. Companion matching is intended for adults; do not use real personal details during review.
